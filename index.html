Welcome to Sydney

CanvasCon ANZ 2026 is almost here, and we can’t wait to welcome you to Sydney.

Whether you’re exploring new approaches to student success, thinking about the role of AI in higher education, or looking for practical ideas you can bring back to campus this fall, this guide will help you get ready for a great week.

Think of it as your one-stop resource for planning your schedule, navigating the conference, and making the most of your time at CanvasCon ANZ.

CanvasCon ANZ takes place 24–25 August 2026 at ILUMINA, 1 Elizabeth Street, Sydney NSW 2000.

Download the app

The Instructure Events app is your home base for everything happening at CanvasCon ANZ.

To get started:

  1. Download Instructure Events from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store (or search “Instructure Events” in your app store).
  2. Log in using the email address you used to register and select Send email link.
  3. Set up your profile with your name, title, organisation, and photo.
  4. Add your LinkedIn profile so other attendees can connect with you.

Not seeing CanvasCon ANZ 2026 in the app? Double check that you’re using the email address you registered with, then uninstall and reinstall the app.

Local logistics

Prepare for Sydney winter weather

August is winter in Sydney, so pack for cool mornings and evenings with generally mild daytime temperatures. Around Sydney Harbour, long-term August averages are approximately 11°C overnight and 18°C during the day.

A few recommendations:

  • Bring comfortable walking shoes.
  • Pack layers you can add or remove throughout the day.
  • A light jacket or coat is a good idea, particularly for mornings and evenings.
  • Bring a compact umbrella in case of rain.
  • Bring a refillable water bottle.
  • Consider an extra light layer for air-conditioned session and meeting spaces.

Getting to ILUMINA

CanvasCon ANZ is being held at ILUMINA, 1 Elizabeth Street, Sydney NSW 2000, in the heart of Sydney’s CBD.

Sydney has an extensive public transport network, making it easy to travel to and from the venue by train, light rail, bus or ferry. If you’re travelling during weekday peak periods, we recommend allowing a little extra time for your journey.

Before you arrive, plan your trip using the Transport for NSW Trip Planner, which provides real-time public transport information, service updates and the best route to the venue.

→ Transport for NSW Trip Planner

If you’re travelling by taxi or rideshare, simply use ILUMINA, 1 Elizabeth Street, Sydney NSW 2000 as your destination.

Filter the agenda

With sessions across Higher Education, K–12 and Vocational Education & Professional Learning, filters are your friend.

To find higher education content:

  1. Open the Agenda page on the web or in the app.
  2. Select the Filter button.
  3. Choose the relevant audience or segment filter.
  4. Select VET, HE or K–12 Stream.

Once you’ve found sessions you’re interested in, log in and star them to build your personal schedule.

Get involved

Some of the best CanvasCon ANZ experiences happen outside the session rooms. Make time to explore the activations across Levels 3 and 4, meet our partners, connect with the Instructure team and get involved in some of the experiences happening throughout the event.

Take the CanvasCon Passport Journey

Pick up your CanvasCon ANZ Passport and use it as your guide to some of the best stops around the event.

Visit each participating booth, chat with the teams, try the experiences and collect a stamp at every stop. Complete all eight stamps and take your finished passport to the Registration Desk to enter the prize draw.

Along the way, you’ll have the chance to:

  • Strike a pose at the CanvasCon Photo Booth and share your experience.
  • Visit the Canvas Product booth to see what’s new, meet our technical experts and explore the latest capabilities.
  • Meet the Instructure Services Team and learn how they support institutions with implementation, training, instructional design and technical expertise.
  • Connect with participating CanvasCon partners and discover more of the tools and services that extend the Canvas experience.

Once you’ve collected every stamp, head to the Registration Desk to enter the draw. Ten winners will be announced during the lunch break on Day 2, with prizes collected from the Registration Desk.
